What it is designed to support

Governance that can be examined.

A practical framework for building observable, auditable AI-assisted processes—not a promise that any system is automatically safe or compliant.

01 / PROVENANCE

Decision evidence trails

Structure records of system events, decision inputs, timestamps, and links between successive audit entries.

02 / OVERSIGHT

Human-gated workflows

Represent a clear boundary between an AI proposal, a human authorization, and any later implementation.

03 / MULTI-AGENT

Disagreement tracking

Compare model outputs, preserve competing interpretations, and make unresolved conflicts visible to reviewers.

04 / ASSURANCE

Audit and incident review

Support post-event reconstruction and structured review, subject to the completeness and trustworthiness of recorded inputs.

05 / DECISION SUPPORT

Operational analysis

Model trade, logistics, implementation options, and commercial scenarios with assumptions separated from observed results.

06 / GOVERNANCE

Four-rail review

Organize evaluation around verification, constraints, transparency, and auditability, with accountable human review.

Operating principle
AI recommends.
Humans decide.
Everything is logged.

Transport is not authority. Integrity is not authorization. A record of an event does not, by itself, prove the event was true.
