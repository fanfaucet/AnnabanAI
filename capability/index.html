Designed for accountable AI workflows

A practical governance layer for teams that need to inspect how AI-supported decisions were formed, what evidence was used, and where human review belongs.

01 / PROVENANCE

Traceable decision records

Structure events, evidence references, timestamps, and decision history to support later review and incident reconstruction.

02 / AUDITABILITY

Integrity-aware logs

Use hash-linked records to help reveal changes in a recorded chain. Integrity checks do not independently prove that an event was true.

03 / HUMAN CONTROL

Authorization boundaries

Separate recommendations, authorization, and external action so that systems do not confuse a model output with permission to act.

04 / MULTI-AGENT REVIEW

Compare and reconcile

Record competing outputs, uncertainty, and disagreements for review instead of silently treating one model as authoritative.

05 / SYSTEMS ENGINEERING

Governance-oriented integration

Plan interfaces, evidence schemas, test harnesses, and review workflows around the requirements of a specific deployment.

06 / COMMERCIAL ANALYSIS

Model viable delivery

Assess implementation scope, pricing assumptions, licensing options, and earning capacity without presenting projections as realized revenue.

From proposal to evidence

A disciplined engagement starts with a bounded use case and produces reviewable deliverables—not unsupported claims of readiness or impact.

01 · Define

Agree on the workflow, risks, scope, success criteria, and human decision owner.

02 · Prototype

Specify event records, provenance links, validation rules, and integration boundaries.

03 · Test

Exercise tampering, missing records, replay, invalid authorization, and failure paths.

04 · Evaluate

Report test results, limitations, implementation effort, and independently measurable outcomes.
